Interpretation of 0.20 percent round-trip:
  0.20 percent is the sum of nominal per-side rates (2 * 0.05 comm +
  2 * 0.05 slip). It is NOT an exact price-move break-even threshold.
  The exact price move needed for zero net PnL is:
    (1 + slip) / (1 - slip) - 1 at fill level, plus commission effect.
  Numerically: (1.0005 / 0.9995 - 1) * 100 = 0.1000 percent per side
  of commission-equivalent, hence ~0.2002 percent round-trip.

Why ordinary TP gross return is not net profitability:
  gross TP price return = 1.003 * 0.9995 - 1 = +0.24985 percent.
  But net PnL also subtracts entry and exit commissions (2 * 0.05 percent
  of notional). So even a TP trade can be net negative if notional is small
  relative to fixed costs and slippage effects compound.

SECTION 7 -- SOURCE-CODE-SPECIFIC DEFECT REVIEW
==============================================================================

A. Equity marking after entry
   status: CONFIRMED DEFECT
   location: backtest_one(), equity block after open_position(i).
   evidence: mark = close[i] used against entry_price = open[i+1]*(1+slip).
   impact: equity_curve and max_dd_pct computed on a curve with transient
           artifact at every entry bar. Trade-level PnL unaffected.
   confidence: high.

B. Last-bar and session-boundary logic
   status: NO DEFECT FOUND (with one modeling note)
   location: session-boundary block, and END_OF_DATA block.
   evidence: position is closed at close[i-1] before new session opens;
             END_OF_DATA closes any open position at last bar's close.
             No path closes the same position twice: after each close
             position is set to None.
   confidence: high.

C. OHLC execution -- intra-bar TP vs SL
   status: CONFIRMED MODELING LIMITATION
   evidence: OHLCV cannot determine intra-bar order. Code uses SL_FIRST=True.
             This is a conservative assumption, not a defect.
   confidence: high.

D. TP gap handling
   status: POTENTIAL ISSUE -- REQUIRES DATA
   evidence: branch order is SL_GAP, TP_GAP, SL_AND_TP, SL, TP.
             If a bar opens >= tp_level AND also touches sl_level,
             TP_GAP branch is evaluated before SL_AND_TP_SAME_BAR.
             That overrides SL-first on such bars.
   quantitative check: see Section 3 (TP_GAP with low <= sl_level).
   confidence: high (code structure), data-dependent for impact.

E. TIME_STOP
   status: NO DEFECT FOUND
   evidence: bars_held = i - entry_index; trigger at bars_held >= H_MAX.
             Trade window spans [entry_index, entry_index + H_MAX] = 13 bars,
             consistent with stated rule.
   confidence: high.

F. Session and data boundaries
   status: NO DEFECT FOUND
   evidence: END_OF_DATA closes any open position at last bar close.
             Signals with no valid next bar -> open_position returns None.
   confidence: high.

G. Capital accounting
   status: CONFIRMED MODELING LIMITATION
   evidence: capital is a PnL accumulator, not a full cash account.
             Position notional is not subtracted from capital on entry.
             Commission is debited at entry; at exit, gross - exit_comm
             is credited. final_capital - INIT - sum(net_pnl) = 0 (verified
             in prior v601 forensic audit).
   impact: modeling limitation, not an internal inconsistency.
   confidence: high.

H. Bootstrap
   status: CONFIRMED MODELING LIMITATION
   evidence: session-block bootstrap over session_key of entry.
             Sessions with one trade contribute a single sample.
             This can produce narrow CI; treat as limitation, not defect.
   confidence: medium-high.
